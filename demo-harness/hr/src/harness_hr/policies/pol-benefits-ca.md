# Benefits Supplement (Canada)

This note adds to the global Benefits Overview for employees whose contract is in Canada. Where the two differ, this note applies.

## Health and dental

Provincial health care is public and is not a company benefit. The company pays extended health and dental at 100 percent for the employee and at 80 percent for enrolled dependants. This is the private cover named in the global overview.

## Pension

The global match of up to 4 percent of base pay is paid into a group RRSP. The employee must contribute to receive the match. Provincial pension plans, where they apply, are separate and are not part of the match.

## Commuting

There is no commuting allowance. Where the province allows it, a transit pass can be bought through payroll as a pre-tax deduction.

## Wellness account

Each employee has a wellness spending account of 500 Canadian dollars per calendar year for fitness, vision, and family care that the health plan does not cover. Unused amounts do not carry over. This account is not the learning budget and not the home-office budget.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
