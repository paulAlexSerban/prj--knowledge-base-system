# Annual Leave Policy (United States)

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in the United States receive 15 working days of paid vacation per calendar year. After 5 complete years the entitlement rises to 20 working days. There is no federal statutory minimum. Company holidays are published each January and are not counted against vacation. Part-time employees who work 20 hours a week or more accrue at half the full-time rate.

## Accrual and carry-over

Vacation accrues monthly, starting on the first day of employment. The balance cannot go above 20 days. Further accrual pauses until the balance drops. Unused days are not paid out when you leave, except in states that require a payout. The People team applies that state rule where it exists.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for a block longer than three days. Your manager answers within five working days.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
