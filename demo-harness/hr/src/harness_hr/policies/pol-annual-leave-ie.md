# Annual Leave Policy (Ireland)

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in Ireland receive 22 working days of paid annual leave per year. The statutory minimum is 20 working days. Public holidays are not counted against this entitlement. Part-time employees accrue leave at 8 percent of hours worked.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days carry over until 31 March of the following year. The rest lapses. The People team sends a reminder every October. Leave is not paid out on resignation except for days you have already accrued and not taken in the current year.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for a block longer than three days. Your manager answers within three working days.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
