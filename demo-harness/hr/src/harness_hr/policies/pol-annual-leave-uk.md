# Annual Leave Policy (United Kingdom)

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in the United Kingdom receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per year, plus the 8 bank holidays in England and Wales. The statutory minimum is 28 days including bank holidays, so this policy is above that minimum. Scotland and Northern Ireland bank holidays are honoured for employees whose contract names that location. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata share of both the 25 days and the bank holidays.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days of the 25 carry over until 31 March of the following year. Bank holidays do not carry over. Leave that sickness stopped you taking can carry further, as the Working Time Regulations require. The People team sends a reminder every October.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for a block longer than five days. Your manager answers within three working days.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
