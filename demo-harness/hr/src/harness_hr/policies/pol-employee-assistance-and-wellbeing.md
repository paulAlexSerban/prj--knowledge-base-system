# Employee Assistance and Wellbeing

## Assistance programme

Every employee can use the employee assistance programme at no cost. It covers up to 6 sessions per year with a counsellor. The sessions are confidential. The provider does not tell the company who attended, and a manager is not informed. Dependants who live with you can use the same 6 sessions.

## Wellbeing days

Every employee receives 3 wellbeing days per calendar year, in addition to annual leave. Request them in the HR system. They cannot be carried over and they are not paid out. They are not a substitute for sick leave.

## What is not included

The programme does not pay for ongoing medical treatment. Longer support goes through the health cover in the Benefits Overview, or through the country benefits supplement where there is one.
