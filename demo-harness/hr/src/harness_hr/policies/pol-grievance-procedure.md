# Grievance Procedure

## When to use this

Use this procedure when you believe a decision, a behaviour, or a working condition is unfair and talking it through has not resolved it. It is not the route for a report of serious misconduct or illegality. Those go through the whistleblowing policy.

## Steps

1. Raise it with your manager. If the grievance is about your manager, start at step 2.
2. Send a written grievance to people@example.com within 10 working days of the event, or of the informal conversation ending without agreement. Say what happened, when, and what you want to change.
3. A People partner who is not in your reporting line meets you within 10 working days. You may bring a colleague. The partner replies in writing within 5 working days of the meeting.
4. You may appeal in writing within 5 working days of that reply. A director who has not been involved decides the appeal and replies within 10 working days. That decision is final inside the company.

## Records

The People team keeps the grievance file for 12 months after the decision. It is not part of your performance record.
