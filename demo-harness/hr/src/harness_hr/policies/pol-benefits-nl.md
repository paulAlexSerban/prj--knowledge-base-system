# Benefits Supplement (Netherlands)

This note adds to the global Benefits Overview for employees whose contract is in the Netherlands. Where the two differ, this note applies.

## Holiday allowance

The company pays a holiday allowance of 8 percent of base pay earned over the previous twelve months. It is paid with the May salary. This is pay, not extra leave. Annual leave days are set in the Netherlands annual leave policy.

## Health

There is no separate private medical policy. The company reimburses the statutory basic health premium up to 150 euros a month for the employee. Dependants are not reimbursed. Dental and physiotherapy above the basic package are not covered.

## Commuting

The company pays the full public-transport commute, or 0.23 euro per kilometre for cycling or driving, up to 30 kilometres one way. Once every three years the company contributes 500 euros toward a bicycle. That contribution is separate from the home-office budget.

## Pension

A Dutch company pension replaces the global 4 percent match. The company pays two thirds of the pension premium and the employee pays one third.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
