# Information Security Policy

## Accounts

Use multi-factor authentication on every company account. Passwords are managed in the company password manager. Share credentials only through it.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and install security updates within seven days. Report a lost device to security@example.com within one hour.

## Incidents

Report suspected phishing or data exposure to security@example.com immediately. Do not investigate on your own. The security team decides on notification of customers and regulators.

## Access

Access to systems follows least privilege. Managers review team access every quarter, and leavers lose access on their last day.
