# Annual Leave Policy (France)

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in France receive 27 working days of paid annual leave per year, from 1 June to 31 May. The statutory minimum is 25 working days. Public holidays are not counted against this entitlement. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their working days.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days carry over until 31 May of the following leave year. Anything above 5 days lapses on 31 May, unless illness or another statutory leave stopped you taking it. The People team sends a reminder every March.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR system at least four weeks ahead for a block longer than one week. Your manager answers within five working days. The main holiday period is between 1 May and 31 October, and the company asks each employee to take at least 10 consecutive working days in that window.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
