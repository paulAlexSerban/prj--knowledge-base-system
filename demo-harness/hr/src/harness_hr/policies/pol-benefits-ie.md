# Benefits Supplement (Ireland)

This note adds to the global Benefits Overview for employees whose contract is in Ireland. Where the two differ, this note applies.

## Health

The company pays the full private health premium for the employee. Dependants can be added at half the extra premium.

## Pension

The company match is 6 percent of base pay, which replaces the global match of 4 percent. The employee contributes at least 3 percent to receive the match.

## Travel

The company pays half the cost of a TaxSaver or Leap card. The cycle-to-work scheme lets you buy a bicycle up to 1,500 euros once every four years, repaid from gross pay. That purchase is separate from the home-office budget.

## Volunteering

Every employee has 1 paid volunteering day per year for a registered charity. It is not a wellbeing day and it does not carry over.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
