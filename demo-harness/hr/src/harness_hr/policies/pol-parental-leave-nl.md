# Parental Leave Policy (Netherlands)

## Eligibility

Every employee in the Netherlands is eligible from their first day of employment. The policy covers birth parents, partners, and adoptive or foster parents. Part-time employees are covered on the same terms.

## Statutory leave

The birth parent has 16 weeks of pregnancy and maternity leave (zwangerschapsverlof and bevallingsverlof). The partner has 1 week of birth leave (geboorteverlof) at full pay and 5 further weeks of additional birth leave paid by the state at 70 percent of salary. Each parent also has 9 weeks of paid parental leave (ouderschapsverlof) in the child's first year, at 70 percent, and unpaid leave on top of that up to 26 times the weekly hours.

## Company pay

The company brings the weeks below to full salary. It does not lengthen the statutory leave.

- The birth parent receives 18 weeks of full salary, which is the statutory 16 weeks plus 2 company weeks.
- The partner receives 9 weeks of full salary in the first year: the statutory week, the 5 additional weeks topped up from 70 percent, and 3 company weeks.
- Adoptive and foster parents receive 6 weeks of full salary from the placement date.

## How to apply

1. Tell your manager and people@example.com at least 6 weeks before the leave starts.
2. The People team confirms the dates and the top-up in writing.
3. The People team files the UWV employer statement within five working days.

## Returning to work

You return to the same role or an equivalent one. You may ask to work fewer hours on return. The manager answers that request within four weeks.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
