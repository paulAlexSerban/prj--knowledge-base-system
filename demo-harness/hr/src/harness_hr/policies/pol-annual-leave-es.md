# Annual Leave Policy (Spain)

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in Spain receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. The statutory minimum is 22 working days. Public holidays, of which there are 14, are not counted against this entitlement. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their working days.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days carry over until 31 March of the following year. Days not taken by then lapse, unless a statutory leave or sick leave made it impossible to take them. The People team sends a reminder every November.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR system at least 15 days ahead for any block. Your manager answers within five working days. Leave dates for the summer are agreed in the team by the end of March.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
