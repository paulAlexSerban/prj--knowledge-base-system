# Parental Leave Policy (United Kingdom)

## Eligibility

All employees in the United Kingdom are eligible from their first day. The policy covers birth parents, partners, and adoptive parents.

## Paid leave

- The birth parent receives 26 weeks of full pay, followed by statutory pay for up to 13 more weeks.
- The partner receives 4 weeks of full pay, taken within the first year.
- Adoptive parents receive 26 weeks of full pay from the placement date.

## Shared parental leave

Parents may share up to 50 weeks of leave and 37 weeks of statutory pay. The company pays the enhanced rate for the first 12 weeks taken by either parent.

## How to apply

Notify your manager and people@example.com at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth.
