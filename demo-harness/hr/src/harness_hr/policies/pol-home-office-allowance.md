# Home Office Allowance

## What the company pays

The remote work policy gives each employee a one-time home-office budget of 500 euros, or the local-currency equivalent. This policy says what that budget covers. It does not add a second budget.

Eligible items are a desk, a chair, a lamp, and a headset. Internet service is not reimbursed.

## How to claim

Claim within 90 days of your start date, or within 90 days of a move to regular remote work. Send the receipt to the People team. The company reimburses the amount on the receipt up to the 500 euro balance. A claim after 90 days is refused.

## Replacing items

You may claim again 4 years after the last payment, and only for an item that is broken or no longer usable. The new claim is again capped at 500 euros. Leaving the company does not require you to return the items.
