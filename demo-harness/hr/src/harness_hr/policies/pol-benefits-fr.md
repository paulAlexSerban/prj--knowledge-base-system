# Benefits Supplement (France)

This note adds to the global Benefits Overview for employees whose contract is in France. Where the two differ, this note applies.

## Health

The global health cover is delivered as a mutuelle. The company pays 60 percent of the employee's premium and 50 percent of a partner's premium. Children listed on the contract are included in the employee share.

## Meals and travel

On days you work, the company provides a meal voucher with a face value of 10 euros. The company pays 6 euros and the employee pays 4 euros, which is taken from net pay. The company also pays 50 percent of a public-transport pass (Navigo or the local equivalent).

## Pension

The global pension match of up to 4 percent of base pay is paid into a company PERECO. It sits on top of the statutory Agirc-Arrco contributions, which are not part of the match.

## What this does not change

The learning budget of 1,500 euros and the three wellbeing days stay as in the global overview.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
