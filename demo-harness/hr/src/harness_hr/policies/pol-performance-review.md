# Performance Review Procedure

## Cycle

Every employee has two reviews a year, in April and in October. The April review sets goals. The October review scores the year so far and resets goals for the next half. New joiners have a check-in at the end of their third month instead of waiting for the next cycle.

## Ratings

The manager records one of three ratings: exceeds, meets, or developing. A rating of developing needs a written plan with two or three concrete actions and a date. The employee sees the rating and the written comments before they are stored, and may add a comment of their own.

## What the review does not do

The review does not set pay. Pay decisions sit in the compensation review, which is a separate process run by the People team. A rating is an input to that process, not the decision.

## Missed reviews

If a manager misses the window, the People partner for the team opens the review and sets a new date within 15 working days. The employee is not marked as developing because a review was late.
