# Disciplinary and Termination Procedure

Restricted to People team administrators and the legal team.

## Stages

1. Informal conversation between the manager and the employee, recorded by the People team.
2. Written warning after an investigation by a People partner who is not in the employee's reporting line.
3. Final written warning, or termination for gross misconduct.

## Rights of the employee

The employee is told the allegation in writing, may bring a colleague or works-council member to any meeting, and may appeal within ten working days.

## Records

Warnings stay on file for 12 months. The People team reviews every termination with Legal before it is communicated.
