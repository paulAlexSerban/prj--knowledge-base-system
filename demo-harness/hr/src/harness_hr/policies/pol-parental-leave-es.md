# Parental Leave Policy (Spain)

## Eligibility

Every employee in Spain is eligible from their first day of employment. The policy covers both birth parents and adoptive or foster parents. Part-time employees are covered on the same terms.

## Statutory leave

Each parent has 16 weeks of birth leave (permiso por nacimiento), paid by social security. The first 6 weeks after the birth are mandatory and must be taken in one block. The remaining 10 weeks can be taken, in weekly blocks, until the child is 12 months old. The weeks cannot be transferred from one parent to the other.

## Company pay

Social security pays a capped daily amount. The company tops that up to full salary and adds extra weeks:

- Each parent receives 16 weeks of full salary.
- The birth parent receives 6 further weeks of full salary, 22 weeks in total, taken within the first year.
- Adoptive and foster parents each receive 16 weeks of full salary from the placement date. There is no extra block.

## How to apply

1. Tell your manager and people@example.com at least 4 weeks before the leave starts, or as soon as you can for an early birth.
2. The People team confirms the dates and the top-up in writing.
3. The People team issues the employer certificate for the social-security claim within five working days.

## Returning to work

You return to the same role or an equivalent one in the same workplace. A return conversation takes place in the week before you come back.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
