# Relocation Procedure

## Company-initiated moves

When the company asks you to move to another country or to an office more than 80 kilometres from your current home, the company pays:

- a lump sum of 8,000 euros, or the local-currency equivalent, toward moving costs,
- two return trips for house hunting, booked through the travel policy,
- temporary housing for up to 30 days in the new location,
- one session with a tax adviser chosen by the company.

The lump sum is paid after you sign the new work location. If you leave the company within 12 months of the move, you repay half of the lump sum.

## Moves you ask for

A move you request, including a move to work remotely from another country for longer than the remote-work policy allows, is not funded. The People team still has to approve it, because of tax and the right to work.

## Family

The lump sum and the temporary housing cover a partner and children who move with you. School fees are not paid.
