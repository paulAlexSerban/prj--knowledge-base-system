# Benefits Overview

## Health

All employees receive private health cover for themselves. Dependants can be added at a reduced premium. Mental-health support is available through an employee assistance programme at no cost.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,500 euros per year for courses, books, and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Pension

Where local law allows, the company matches pension contributions up to 4 percent of base pay.

## Wellbeing days

Every employee receives three wellbeing days per year, in addition to annual leave. The employee assistance programme, the learning budget, and the home-office allowance each have their own policy.

## Country rules

France, the Netherlands, Spain, Ireland, and Canada each have a benefits supplement. Where a supplement differs from this page, the supplement applies. Annual leave and parental leave depend on the country of employment.
