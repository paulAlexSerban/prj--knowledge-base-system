# Redundancy Procedure

Restricted to People team administrators and the legal team.

## When this applies

This procedure applies when a role is removed because the work has stopped or reduced, not because of the employee's conduct or performance. Conduct and performance follow the disciplinary procedure.

## Consultation

The People team and Legal lead the consultation. The employee is told in writing that the role is at risk, why, and how long the consultation lasts. The consultation is at least 15 working days, and longer where local law requires it. The employee may bring a colleague or a representative to any meeting.

## Selection

Where several people do the same work and not all roles are removed, selection uses the skills the remaining work needs. Length of service is not the only criterion. The People team checks the outcome for a disparate effect on a protected group before anyone is dismissed.

## Notice and support

Notice follows the contract and local law, whichever is longer. Employees with 2 or more years of service receive outplacement support for 3 months. Severance is at least the statutory amount. The company adds 2 weeks of base pay per complete year of service, capped at 26 weeks, and offsets that addition against any statutory payment that covers the same period.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
