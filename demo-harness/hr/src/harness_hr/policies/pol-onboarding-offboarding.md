# Onboarding and Offboarding Procedure

## Joining

Before the first day the People team sends the contract, the start date, and the name of a buddy. On the first day the employee receives a laptop, access to email, and the employee handbook. The buddy meets them in the first week and stays the point of contact for 30 days.

The manager sets three goals for the first 90 days and writes them down. A check-in at the end of the third month replaces the next performance review if that review would fall inside the first 90 days.

## Leaving

The manager tells the People team the last working day as soon as it is agreed. Access to email, code, and the HR system is removed at the end of that day. The employee returns the laptop and any other equipment within 5 working days. The company pays the cost of returning it.

The People team offers an exit conversation in the last week. It is optional. Notes from it are not shared with the manager in a form that names the employee.

## What is paid on leaving

Unused annual leave is paid only where the country policy or local law says so. The country annual leave policy is the source for that rule.
