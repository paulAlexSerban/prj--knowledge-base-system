# Benefits Supplement (Spain)

This note adds to the global Benefits Overview for employees whose contract is in Spain. Where the two differ, this note applies.

## Health

The company pays private medical cover for the employee and for children under 26. A partner can be added. The employee pays the partner's premium in full.

## Meals and travel

On days you work, the company provides restaurant vouchers of 11 euros, fully company paid. The company also pays 50 percent of a monthly travel pass, capped at 60 euros a month.

## Pension

Local tax rules cap the company pension contribution at 1 percent of base pay. That 1 percent replaces the global match of 4 percent. The company does not pay the difference as cash.

## Moving house

You may take 2 paid days when you change your primary home. Tell your manager at least one week ahead. These days are not annual leave and do not come out of the wellbeing days.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
