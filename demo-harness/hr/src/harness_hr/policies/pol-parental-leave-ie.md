# Parental Leave Policy (Ireland)

## Eligibility

Every employee in Ireland is eligible from their first day of employment. The policy covers birth parents, partners, and adoptive parents. Part-time employees receive pay in proportion to their usual hours.

## Statutory leave

The birth parent has 26 weeks of maternity leave, plus 16 further weeks of unpaid maternity leave. Maternity Benefit is a flat weekly state payment, not full pay. The partner has 2 weeks of paternity leave. Each parent has 9 weeks of parent's leave, paid at the Parent's Benefit rate, to be taken in the first 2 years, and 26 weeks of unpaid parental leave per child until the child is 12.

## Company pay

The company pays full salary for the blocks below. State benefits are offset, not added on top.

- The birth parent receives 28 weeks of full salary. The remaining statutory unpaid maternity leave stays unpaid.
- The partner receives 2 weeks of full salary, taken within 6 months of the birth.
- Adoptive parents receive 24 weeks of full salary from the placement date.

## How to apply

1. Tell your manager and people@example.com at least 6 weeks before the leave starts.
2. The People team confirms the dates and the company pay in writing.
3. The People team completes the employer section of the state benefit form within five working days.

## Returning to work

You return to the same role or a suitable alternative on terms no less favourable. Tell the People team at least 4 weeks before your return date if you want to change your hours.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
