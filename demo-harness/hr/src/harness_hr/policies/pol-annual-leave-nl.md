# Annual Leave Policy (Netherlands)

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in the Netherlands receive 26 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. The statutory minimum is 20 working days. Public holidays are not counted against this entitlement. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their hours.

## Holiday allowance

The company pays a holiday allowance (vakantiegeld) of 8 percent of base pay earned in the previous twelve months. It is paid with the May salary. It is not extra leave.

## Carry-over

The statutory 20 days expire 6 months after the end of the year they were earned, unless you could not take them. The extra 6 company days carry over until 31 December of the following year and then lapse. The People team sends a reminder every September.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR system at least two weeks ahead. Your manager answers within three working days and may refuse only when the timing would seriously disrupt the team.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
