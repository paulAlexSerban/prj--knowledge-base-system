# Annual Leave Policy (Canada)

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in Canada receive 18 working days of paid vacation per calendar year. After 3 complete years the entitlement rises to 23 working days. Public holidays follow the province of employment and are not counted against vacation. Part-time employees accrue vacation pay at the same rate as their full-time peers.

## Carry-over and payout

Up to 5 unused days carry into the next calendar year. Days above that are paid out in January. On termination the company pays out unused vacation, which provincial law requires. The People team sends a reminder every November.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for a block longer than five days. Your manager answers within three working days.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
