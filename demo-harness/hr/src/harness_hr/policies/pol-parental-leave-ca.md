# Parental Leave Policy (Canada)

## Eligibility

Employees in Canada are eligible after 90 days of employment. The policy covers birth parents, second parents, and adoptive parents. Part-time employees receive pay in proportion to their usual hours. Provincial employment standards apply where they are more generous than this policy.

## Statutory leave

Employment Insurance pays maternity benefits for up to 15 weeks at 55 percent of insurable earnings, and parental benefits that parents can share. The standard option is up to 40 weeks at 55 percent, with one parent taking at most 35. The extended option is up to 69 weeks at 33 percent. Job protection follows the province of employment and is longer than the paid benefit in most provinces.

## Company pay

The company tops benefits up to full salary for a shorter block. Choose the standard or the extended government option before the leave starts. The company top-up applies only to the standard option.

- The birth parent receives 15 weeks of full salary.
- Either parent receives a further 10 weeks of full salary, which the two parents may split.
- Adoptive parents receive 12 weeks of full salary from the placement date.

## How to apply

1. Give 4 weeks' notice where you can. Send the dates to your manager and to people@example.com.
2. The People team confirms the top-up in writing and issues the record of employment within five working days of the last paid day.
3. Benefits continue during the paid block at the same employer contribution.

## Returning to work

You return to the same role or a comparable one. Confirm your return date with your manager at least 4 weeks ahead.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
