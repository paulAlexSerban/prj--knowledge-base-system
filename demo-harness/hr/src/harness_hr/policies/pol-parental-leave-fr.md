# Parental Leave Policy (France)

## Eligibility

Every employee in France is eligible from their first day of employment. The policy covers birth parents, second parents, and adoptive parents. Part-time employees are covered on the same terms, with pay in proportion to their usual hours.

## Statutory leave

French law gives the birth parent 16 weeks of maternity leave for the first and second child, and 26 weeks from the third child. The second parent has 25 days of paternity leave, of which 4 days are mandatory. Either parent may then take congé parental, which is job-protected and unpaid, for up to one year and can be renewed until the child is three.

## Company pay

The company pays full salary on top of the daily social-security allowance:

- The birth parent receives 20 weeks of full salary.
- The second parent receives 5 weeks of full salary, taken within 6 months of the birth.
- Adoptive parents receive 16 weeks of full salary from the placement date.

Congé parental stays unpaid. The company does not extend it beyond the statutory length.

## How to apply

1. Tell your manager and people@example.com the expected date and the weeks you want, at least 8 weeks before the leave starts.
2. The People team confirms the dates and the company top-up in writing.
3. The People team sends the employer certificate for the social-security claim within five working days.

## Returning to work

You return to the same role or an equivalent one in the same team and location. A return-to-work conversation takes place two weeks before your return date.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
