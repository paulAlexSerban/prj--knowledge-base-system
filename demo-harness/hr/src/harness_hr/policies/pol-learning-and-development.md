# Learning and Development Policy

## Budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,500 euros per calendar year, as the Benefits Overview states. It pays for courses, books, examinations, and conference tickets that relate to the current role or to a goal written in the performance review. Unused budget does not carry over, and it is not paid out.

## Approval

A manager approves any single item over 500 euros before you buy it. Items at or under 500 euros need a receipt sent to the People team within 30 days. Travel to a conference follows the travel and expense policy and comes out of this budget, not out of a separate one.

## Time

You may use up to 5 working days per year for study and examinations. Those days are not annual leave and they do not carry over. Agree the dates with your manager at least two weeks ahead.
