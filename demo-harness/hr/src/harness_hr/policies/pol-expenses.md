# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking

Book flights and hotels through the travel tool. Economy class applies to flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for longer flights with manager approval.

## Daily limits

- Meals while travelling: 60 euros per day, or the local equivalent.
- Hotel: up to 180 euros per night in major cities and 130 euros elsewhere.
- Ground transport: public transport or standard taxi. Car rental needs approval.

## Claiming

Submit expenses within 30 days with receipts for every item over 25 euros. Managers approve within five working days and Finance pays in the next payroll run.

## Not reimbursed

Alcohol beyond a single drink with a client meal, personal entertainment, fines, and upgrades paid for personal preference are not reimbursed.
