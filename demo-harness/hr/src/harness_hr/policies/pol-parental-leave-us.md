# Parental Leave Policy (United States)

## Eligibility

Employees in the United States are eligible after 90 days of employment. The policy covers birth parents, second parents, and adoptive or foster parents.

## Paid leave

- The birth parent receives 12 weeks of paid leave at full pay.
- The second parent or adoptive parent receives 6 weeks of paid leave at full pay.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Additional unpaid leave

The Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) gives eligible employees up to 12 weeks of job-protected unpaid leave in a 12-month period. Paid leave under this policy runs concurrently with FMLA leave.

## How to apply

Give 30 days' notice where possible. Send the request to your manager and to people@example.com. Benefits continue during leave at the same employer contribution.
