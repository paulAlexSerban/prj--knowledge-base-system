# Whistleblowing Policy

## What to report

Report a reasonable suspicion of illegal activity, a serious breach of the code of conduct, or a risk to health and safety. Ordinary disagreements about a decision go through the grievance procedure, not this one.

## How to report

Write to people@example.com or to legal@example.com, or use the anonymous form linked from the intranet. You do not have to give your name. If you do, it is shared only with the people who investigate.

## What happens next

The People team or Legal acknowledges the report within 7 days. They tell you the outcome, or why they cannot, within 3 months. Investigation notes are kept for 3 years and are not placed on the reporter's personnel file.

## No retaliation

Retaliation against a person who reports in good faith, or who helps an investigation, is misconduct and is handled under the disciplinary procedure. A report that you know to be false is also misconduct.
