# Sick Leave Policy

## Pay

The company pays full salary for the first 10 working days of sickness in a calendar year. After that, pay follows the statutory sick pay or insurance scheme of the country of employment. Those local rules are more generous in some countries, and they apply when they are.

Part-time employees receive the same 10 days, paid for the hours they were scheduled to work.

## Telling your manager

Tell your manager before the start of your working day, or as soon as you can. You do not need to describe the illness. A medical certificate is required from the fourth calendar day of a continuous absence.

## Longer absences

If you are still absent after 10 working days, the People team writes to you with the local pay rule and a contact. A return-to-work conversation happens on the day you come back when the absence was longer than one week.

Figures in this policy are illustrative and are not legal advice. Local law prevails where it is more generous.
