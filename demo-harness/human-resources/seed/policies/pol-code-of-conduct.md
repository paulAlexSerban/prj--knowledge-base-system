# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are not tolerated in any form, in person or online.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose any outside role, investment, or relationship that could influence your decisions. Gifts above 100 euros must be declined or reported to your manager.

## Reporting a concern

Report concerns to your manager, to the People team at people@example.com, or anonymously through the ethics line. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is a disciplinary matter.

## Data and confidentiality

Customer and employee data may be used only for the purpose it was collected for. Do not share it outside the company without approval from Legal.
