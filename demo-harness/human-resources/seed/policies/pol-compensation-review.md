# Compensation Review Guidelines

Restricted to People team administrators.

## Cycle

Reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Managers submit proposals through the People partner for their department.

## Principles

Pay decisions rest on role scope, performance, and the market range for the role and region. The People team checks every proposal for pay-equity gaps before approval.

## Process

The People partner calibrates proposals across the department, then the department head and the People director approve. Employees are told in a one-to-one conversation by their manager.

## Confidentiality

Individual pay information is never stored in the knowledge system. Only this process description is.
