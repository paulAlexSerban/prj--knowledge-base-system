# Remote Work Policy

## Principle

Work is something you do, not a place you go. Teams choose how often they meet in person, and the minimum is two team days per quarter.

## Working from another country

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year with your manager's approval. Longer periods need approval from the People team because of tax and social-security rules. Employees must not work from sanctioned countries.

## Equipment and allowance

The company provides a laptop, a monitor, and a one-time home-office budget of 500 euros or the local equivalent. Internet costs are not reimbursed.

## Security

Use the company VPN on public networks. Do not store company data on personal devices.
