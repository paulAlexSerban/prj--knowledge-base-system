# Annual Leave Policy (Germany)

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in Germany receive 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Public holidays are not counted against this entitlement. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their working days.

## Carry-over

Unused leave carries over until 31 March of the following year. After that date it lapses, unless illness or leave prevented you from taking it. The People team sends a reminder every October.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for blocks longer than five days. Your manager answers within three working days.
